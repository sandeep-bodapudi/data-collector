📁 Your Excel files

    Collects only public pages that don't need a login. It follows each website's rules (robots.txt) and waits between visits. Collected emails and phone numbers are personal data, so use them in line with company policy and privacy law. No spam.

    How it works

    1. 1
      Choose what to collect

      Search the web finds details on websites (emails, phones, addresses…). Find places gives you a list of places, like all temples in a city.

    2. 2
      Fill in the steps

      Type your search or location and press Enter. Tick the details you need. You can skip the optional steps.

    3. 3
      Press "Start collecting"

      You'll see live progress. A big search can take a few minutes, and you can stop anytime and still keep what was found.

    4. 4
      Download your Excel file

      All your files stay under Your Excel files at the bottom of the page.

    💡
    Tips: add words like "contact" or "email" to your search to find more contact details. Use several smaller searches instead of one very broad one.
